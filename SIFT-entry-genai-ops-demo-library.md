[SITUATION]
The AWS GenAI Operations Demos Library (Pathfinder product owned by Benjamin Lecoq) launched in February 2026 as a collection of ready-to-present demonstrations enabling TAMs to showcase GenAI applications in cloud operations during customer calls. While the underlying code samples existed on GitHub, the library lacked interactive, self-guided demos that TAMs could share directly with customers or use as presentation aids without deploying infrastructure. The program, ideated in EMEA and expanding globally, needed polished interactive walkthroughs to enable TAMs to lead GenAI conversations with their customers across NAMER, APAC, and EMEA.

[ACTIONS]
The TAM created 9 interactive Storylane demonstrations covering GenAI operational use cases including DevOps Agent incident investigation, runtime migration, cost optimization, documentation generation, and chaos engineering. For each demonstration, the TAM deployed the code sample end-to-end, validated functionality, built step-by-step interactive walkthroughs, and filed bugs and improvements through the product Slack channel. The demos were then presented by Cloud Operations Architects during Paris Summit week on the Enterprise Support kiosk and generated strong interest from TAMs looking to use them with their customers. The TAM also contributed to presentations at NAMER, APAC, and EMEA Growth Days to drive TAM adoption.

[RESULTS]
The 9 demonstrations achieved 529 total views with 72% engagement rate. TAMs across NAMER, APAC, and EMEA have begun exploring the demonstrations for customer use during cadence calls, contributing to the business goal of scaling GenAI and DevOps Agent adoption through TAM-led customer conversations. The TAM continues building additional demonstrations and collecting feedback from TAMs on requested topics.
